Section 2 — Forward Projection

How it works: Enter your ROE (%) forecasts for each bank below. The optimizer uses an iterative active-set method to find the exact long-only Markowitz Tangency Portfolio. Starting with all 9 banks, it solves the unconstrained tangency problem on a sub-covariance matrix, removes any bank with a negative raw weight, and repeats until all remaining weights are non-negative. It then applies thesis zero-weight overrides for dominated banks (Berhan, Zemen, BOA) and renormalizes weights to sum to 1. Finally it recomputes expected return, σ, and the Sharpe ratio. Note: The covariance matrix (correlations) is kept constant from the historical 2017/18–2023/24 period. This assumes that the relationships between bank returns remain stable. ROE inputs are capped at 30% (historical max was ~26%).
